# Daily report — 2026-11-07

**Summary:** Milestone 1 is complete: one drivable tank and breakable buildings. After that, a round of feel tuning and strict type checking. Every piece was playtested in Studio via the MCP. No console errors came from game code; the only output was Roblox's own avatar-asset warnings.

## Shipped

### Project setup
- `CLAUDE.md` holds the project rules: all logic in `src/`, server-authoritative, `--!strict`, tunables in `Config.luau`, playtest after every change.
- luau-lsp 1.70.1 (via rokit) runs a strict type check with `scripts/typecheck.sh`. It's clean across `src/` and now required before playtesting.

### Tank
- The tank is built in code from basic parts in a toy style (rounded, bright colours). A player gets one when they join and is seated as the driver.
- W/S drive and A/D rotate the hull, using physics constraints. The driver's client simulates the tank, so controls respond instantly.
- The turret follows the mouse at a capped speed of 80°/s. The barrel tilts on its own hinge, from −7° to +22°.
- A third-person camera follows the hull and pulls back and up as speed increases.

### Shooting
- Clicking fires. The server checks the 2s cooldown, aims along its own copy of the barrel, and ignores any arguments the client sends. In testing, 10 spammed requests produced 1 shot.
- Every player's game draws the tracer, muzzle flash and impact burst from the server's broadcast.
- The HUD shows a crosshair, a marker for where the barrel actually points, and a reload bar.

### Destruction
- Buildings are generated pre-broken: walls and roof are grids of chunks welded to their neighbours, sitting on a foundation that can't move.
- A shell breaks the chunks inside the blast radius and throws them outward.
- Damaged walls sag: a chunk with nothing beneath it falls once fewer than 3 welds hold it, and this can chain up to 4 times.
- Loose debris fades out after 8s, with a hard cap of 500 pieces.
- The map has 3 buildings (Hut 59, Shop 105, Warehouse 203 chunks) and 5 indestructible cover blocks.
- `MapService.RoundReset()` rebuilds the whole map in about 42ms.

### Tuning pass
- **Barrel elevation:** shells can now hit the ground and targets above or below barrel height.
- **Tank can tilt over obstacles:** a soft self-righting force replaced the lock that kept the tank perfectly level. Invisible ramped skids let it climb a 1-stud ledge (13.7° tilt).
- **Steering** is a torque the client sets each frame, adjusted for frame rate. It reaches 1.56 rad/s against a 1.48 target and stops crisply.
- **Blasts:** impulse 65 → 90, upward push 0.35 → 0.5.
- **Windows** are staggered and kept out of door columns, so intact buildings are structurally sound under the sag rule.

## Issues found and fixed
- **Controls never bound:** the place has no default `PlayerModule`, so waiting for it hung the client. The wait is now optional.
- **Tank stuck on a ledge:** it wedged against the 1-stud SpawnLocation pad. The fix was the soft upright force plus ramped skids, and the stray SpawnLocation was deleted in Studio.
- **Steering jitter:** the first torque controller oscillated because Studio renders at about 15 fps when unfocused. It was replaced with a version that adjusts for frame time.
- **Chunks falling unprompted:** intact buildings had chunks held only from the sides (between stacked windows, and above doors). Window layout was fixed.

## Open items
